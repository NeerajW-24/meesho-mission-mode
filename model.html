Reality checks

  • Meesho has 274M annual transacting users (Q1 FY27); 54.22% of FY25 consumers were women, so roughly 125M male buyers. 23.5M new active male buyers by FY31 is about 19% of that base.
  • Meesho added about 62M net annual buyers in the last year (274M at +29% YoY). Mission Start's ~5–6M extra male first orders a year is about a 20% lift on the ~28M men among them.
  • 1.5 lakh Dukaan QR shops by FY31 is about 3.7% of Kirana Club's 4.1M retailers. Amazon's Local Shops programme passed 1 lakh stores.
  • Peak investment of ~₹35 cr is about 0.5% of Meesho's ₹6,521 cr cash. FY31 profit impact is about 1.2% of FY26 revenue.
  • Not counted (upside): fewer wrong-item claims, higher AOV from the mission price ladder, Vaani conversion on mission sessions, seller ad spend on mission shelves.
